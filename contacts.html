For students

Students interested in device physics, 2D materials, nanofabrication and low-temperature transport are welcome to write. Send a CV and a short note on what you would like to work on — see open positions.

Sample and measurement requests

If you would like us to characterise a device, please include the following in your first email. It saves a round trip.

  • Sample material and layer structure
  • Device geometry and channel dimensions
  • What you need measured — transfer and output curves, low-temperature transport, noise, or high-field measurements
  • Process information, optical images or earlier data you already have
  • Timeline, and what the result is for
