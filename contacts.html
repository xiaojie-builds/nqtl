Admission requirements

  • Outstanding undergraduates from a Project 985 or 211 university, recommended for graduate study without the entrance exam, in chemistry, physics, materials science, electronics, optics or a related field.
  • A genuine drive for research, with a dependable, self-motivated and creative approach.
  • A College English Test (CET-6) score of 450 or above. Prior research experience is an advantage.

By metro

Line 2 to Jiayuan Road Station (佳园路站), then walk about ten minutes to the East Campus gate. The center is inside the campus, a few minutes further on.

Campus access

The center sits inside the HUST East Campus. Visitors and vehicles need to be registered at the gate, so write to us a day ahead and we will arrange entry and meet you.
