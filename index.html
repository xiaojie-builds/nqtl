About us

Welcome to Xuefei Li's research group at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. Our group advances the foundations of next-generation electronics by bridging emerging materials, fundamental quantum physics, advanced semiconductor devices, and heterogeneous integration. By combining materials innovation, device physics, and advanced fabrication, we push past the limits of conventional semiconductor technology and build new hardware platforms for future information processing.
